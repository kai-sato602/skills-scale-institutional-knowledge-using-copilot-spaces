# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Technical Leads/Architects** for design guidance and code reviews
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Professionals** to validate acceptance criteria and resolve defects
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on timeline estimates and blockers
- Receive work prioritization from **Product Managers** via the backlog
- Report technical risks to **Technical Leads** and escalate through **Project Managers** if needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Managers** to ensure delivery timelines are realistic and dependencies are managed
- Work with **Developers** to define acceptance criteria and refine scope
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Report progress and metrics to **Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders**
- Coordinate with **Security/Compliance Leads** on security and compliance requirements early in planning

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Sync weekly with **Product Managers** on priorities and scope changes
- Collect status and blockers from **Developers**, **Technical Leads**, and **QA/Testing Professionals**
- Escalate risks and blockers to **Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders** and dependent teams
- Work with **Scrum Masters** to remove impediments and maintain team velocity
- Ensure **Security/Compliance Leads** have visibility into timelines and can integrate compliance checks

---

## QA/Testing Professional

### Role Summary
QA/Testing professionals ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies, acceptance validation, and quality standards enforcement. They collaborate with developers and product teams to validate that features meet acceptance criteria and performance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before features move to production
- Report bugs with reproduction steps and severity levels
- Participate in release validation and smoke testing
- Maintain test documentation and metrics

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Enable fast, confident deployments through comprehensive testing
- Provide objective evidence of readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA sign-offs in sprint planning
- Bug reports and test result summaries
- Participation in release checklists and deployment meetings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work closely with **Developers** to understand implementation details and resolve defects
- Review acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** to ensure comprehensive test coverage
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on testing strategy for complex features
- Provide quality metrics and release readiness assessments to **Project Managers**
- Collaborate with **Security/Compliance Leads** on security testing and compliance validation
- Participate in release validation with the **Technical Lead/Architect** and **Project Manager**

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and risk mitigation. They mentor developers, drive architectural decisions, and ensure technical solutions align with long-term scalability and maintainability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers and conduct code reviews
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Provide estimates and feasibility assessments for complex work
- Collaborate with product teams on technical trade-offs

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Accelerate team velocity through strong engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Risk assessments during planning and execution

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on technical design and mentor through code reviews
- Advise **Product Managers** on feasibility and technical trade-offs for features
- Partner with **Project Managers** to surface technical risks and dependencies early
- Review testing strategy and participate in release validation with **QA/Testing Professionals**
- Consult with **Security/Compliance Leads** on secure architecture and data protection patterns
- Lead technical problem-solving with **Scrum Masters** when blockers arise

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and funding/resource authorization. They represent business interests and ensure projects align with organizational goals and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project initiation and resource allocation
- Make trade-off decisions between scope, timeline, and resources
- Provide escalation authority for business-impacting issues
- Communicate project status to broader organization
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI from projects
- Ensure strategic alignment and risk management
- Enable timely decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and business case reviews
- Monthly status updates and milestone achievements
- Escalations and trade-off decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Approve project plans and resource allocations proposed by **Project Managers**
- Receive strategic briefings and success metrics from **Product Managers**
- Participate in escalation calls for business-impacting risks or scope changes initiated by **Project Managers**
- Receive updates on quality and readiness from **QA/Testing Professionals** and **Technical Leads**
- Make strategic decisions on security and compliance trade-offs in coordination with **Security/Compliance Leads**

---

## Security/Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and compliance leads ensure that projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and data protection policies. They advise on secure design patterns and manage incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and threat models
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews and scanning
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Advise on secure coding and data handling practices
- Manage security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Maintain security documentation and runbooks

### Goals
- Protect customer data and organizational assets
- Ensure compliance with regulations and standards
- Enable secure, rapid delivery without cutting corners

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning
- Security incident escalations and response coordination
- Post-incident retrospectives and action items

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** early in planning to identify security and compliance requirements
- Review designs and code with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** to ensure secure architecture
- Work with **QA/Testing Professionals** to validate security controls and compliance checks
- Report security risks and compliance status to **Project Managers** for escalation if needed
- Advise **Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders** on regulatory and risk implications
- Collaborate with **Scrum Masters** to integrate security into team ceremonies and workflows

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices. They help teams optimize their delivery process and maintain sustainable velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Maintain project board and sprint artifacts
- Optimize team processes based on retrospective feedback
- Escalate systemic blockers to leadership

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery predictability
- Foster continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Remove non-technical barriers to progress

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremony facilitation and notes
- Impediment tracking and status updates
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Developers**, **Technical Leads**, and **QA/Testing Professionals** in removing blockers and optimizing workflow
- Facilitate communication between **Product Managers** and the delivery team on backlog prioritization
- Partner with **Project Managers** to surface systemic impediments and track action items
- Coach the team on collaboration and psychological safety practices
- Work with all roles to drive continuous improvement and process optimization based on retrospective feedback
